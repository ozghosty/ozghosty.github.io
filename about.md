G'day, I'm OzGhosty, a Manly Beach local.

Most mornings I walk the beach from South Steyne to Queenscliff and film it for the daily **Manly Beach Report**: what's on in Manly that day, Northern Beaches news, the stories behind the buildings and landmarks, and the surf and weather. Watch it on [YouTube]({{ site.social.youtube }}), catch the 60-second version on [TikTok]({{ site.social.tiktok }}) or [Instagram]({{ site.social.instagram }}), or read every episode's transcript on the [blog]({{ '/tags/' | relative_url }}). All the links are on one page: [ozghosty.github.io/links]({{ '/links/' | relative_url }}).

This blog is my notebook for building a calmer, healthier and more curious life. I write about the things I'm learning and experimenting with:

- **Tech**: home labs, self-hosting, AI tools and the occasional side project.
- **Health & longevity**: sleep, movement, nutrition and the small habits that compound.
- **Nature & travel**: weekend escapes, big landscapes and getting outside more.
- **Building things**: VR worlds, game dev and creative experiments.
- **The long game**: investing, simplifying and working toward a smaller footprint and an off-grid future.

I'm not an expert in most of this. I'm just figuring it out in public, and hopefully some of it is useful to you too.

If you want to follow along, grab the [RSS feed]({{ '/feed.xml' | relative_url }}).
