There's a lot of noise in the longevity space. Expensive gadgets, complicated protocols, endless supplements. Most of what actually works is boring, and that's good news, because boring is repeatable.

*I'm not a doctor and none of this is medical advice. It's just what I'm doing.*

## The big four

### 1. Sleep

Same bedtime, same wake time, dark cool room. Sleep is the foundation that makes everything else easier.

### 2. Steps

I aim for 10,000 a day, but the real goal is to *never sit for too long*. Walking meetings, walking calls, walking after meals.

### 3. Nutrition

Mostly whole foods, plenty of protein and plants, not much that comes in a crinkly packet. Nothing fancy.

### 4. Strength

Two or three short sessions a week. Muscle is one of the best long-term investments you can make.

## Supplements

I keep it minimal: vitamin D in winter, creatine and magnesium. Talk to your GP before adding anything.

## The real secret

Consistency beats intensity. A good habit you do for ten years beats a perfect one you quit in ten days.
