Most VR is loud: shooters, rhythm games, things jumping at you. I wanted the opposite. A place you can put the headset on and just *breathe*.

## The idea

A small collection of calm, explorable spaces:

- An overwater hut at dusk with lanterns and gentle waves
- A Japanese garden with a koi pond and falling leaves
- A quiet forest clearing under the stars

No goals, no timers. Just presence.

## The toolkit

- **Unity** for the scenes, lighting and interaction
- **Claude Code** for writing and refactoring C# scripts, which speeds up the boring parts enormously
- **ComfyUI** for generating textures, skyboxes and concept art

## Lessons so far

1. **Performance is everything.** A beautiful scene that drops frames feels awful in a headset.
2. **Audio does half the work.** Spatial sound makes a space feel real.
3. **Less is more.** Fewer, better details beat a cluttered scene.
