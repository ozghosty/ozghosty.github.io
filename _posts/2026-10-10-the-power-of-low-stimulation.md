At some point I noticed I couldn't sit still without reaching for my phone. Waiting for the kettle, standing in a queue, even walking. Every gap got filled.

So I started an experiment: deliberately make life *less* stimulating.

## What I changed

- **No phone for the first hour of the day.** Coffee, light and quiet first.
- **Notifications off** for everything except actual people.
- **Walks without headphones**, at least some of the time.
- **Boredom on purpose.** Let the mind wander instead of reaching for input.

## What happened

The first week was uncomfortable. Then things got noticeably better. Ideas started showing up in the gaps. Focus got easier. Evenings felt longer.

It turns out creativity needs empty space to grow in.

## Try it

Pick one gap in your day and leave it empty for a week. That's it.
