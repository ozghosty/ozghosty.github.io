Some weekends you need to get out of your own head. This was one of them.

No big plan, no itinerary, just a backpack, a full water bottle and a loose idea of heading north along the coast until something looked good.

## Start early, stay slow

The best part of any coastal walk is the first hour after sunrise. The light is soft, the tracks are empty and the water looks unreal. I started with a coffee by the beach and then just walked. Headland to headland, stopping whenever a view deserved it (which was often).

## A few favourites

- **The headland walks**: short, steep in places, and worth every step for the views back down the coast.
- **Rock pools at low tide**: ten minutes of looking closely is better than any screen.
- **A long lunch somewhere with a view**: fish, chips and zero rush.
- **Sunset from a high point**: I always bring a layer. It gets cold faster than you expect.

## Why it matters

It's easy to live close to places like this and never actually go. A weekend like this resets everything: sleep is better, my head is clearer and I come back with more ideas than I left with.

Next time I'm going to try doing the whole thing without my phone. We'll see how that goes.
