Every big change starts as a daydream. This one has been on my mind for years: a patch of land, a small house and a life that runs on sunshine and rainwater.

## The vision

- **Around 20 acres.** Enough room for a food forest, a few animals and space to breathe.
- **Solar and batteries** for power.
- **Rainwater tanks** and good water management.
- **A small, efficient home**, not a mansion.

## Where?

We're exploring options across three states:

| State | Pros | Cons |
| --- | --- | --- |
| NSW | Close to family, varied climate | Pricier land |
| QLD | Warm, long growing season | Heat, cyclones up north |
| VIC | Rainfall, cooler climate | Bushfire risk in some areas |

## The plan

Save, research, visit and talk to people who've done it. No rush. This is a five-year project, not a five-week one.

I'll document the journey here, mistakes included.
